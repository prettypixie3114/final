Section 4 of 6
Root Cause Analysis — Why Targets Were Missed
The plan was sound. The execution gaps are specific and diagnosable. Here is the ranked order of causes behind the MER shortfall.
Root Cause 01 · Highest Impact
Legacy campaigns never paused — 82% of spend in dead accounts
Revenue loss: ₹2.8L over 20 days at blended 0.84× ROAS vs 1.4× floor
Sheet 14 W1 Roadmap was explicit: "Kill all 10 STOP campaigns." The execution checklist shows this as Day 1, unmarked. Seven legacy campaigns (SSTLAL, AD+CR+NCR, SINP-09-Apr, SSTCLBS, Sales|IN%, RM|Nov-25, AD+CR|SINP-Apr) consumed ₹8.38L — 82% of the ₹10.24L total account spend. At 50% GM, this spend generated negative contribution on 5 of 7 campaigns. The new 9-campaign structure had only ₹1.46L to work with. The plan required a clean slate; execution never created one.
Root Cause 02 · Highest Impact
Catalog feed setup delayed C7 by 10 days — highest-ROAS slot ran empty
Opportunity cost: ~₹80K in C7 revenue at 2.5–3.0× projected ROAS
The plan in Sheet 14 W3 stated "RT-7day-DPA campaign live" and noted catalog as a "gating — Week 1 priority." Evidence of failure: original C7 launched May 9 with CPM ₹1,759 (no catalog → tiny audience), only 2,187 impressions in 5 days. This is the fingerprint of a DPA campaign with no product feed. ₹8K daily budget was planned for C7 — only ₹384/day effective spend was reached. The catalog was correctly configured by May 19 (CPM dropped to ₹12) but this cost 10 days of the most profitable planned campaign.
Root Cause 03 · High Impact
6 of 9 planned campaigns never launched in Phase 1
Budget gap: ₹21K/day in planned retargeting/reactivation spend went to legacy waste
C3 (Rest-of-India Prepaid), C4 (F25-34), C5 (LAL Refresh), C6 (Premium Anchor), C8 (RT Warm 30-70d), and C9 (Past-Buyer Reactivation) all show 0 spend in the period. These represent the entire retention and reactivation stack. The plan's path to 5× MER was built on these campaigns contributing 28% of spend at 1.7–5.5× ROAS. Their absence means the blended MER has no high-ROAS anchors to pull it toward the 1.6× target. Prerequisite blockers: PixieCredits system, UGC review collection, premium photography, and LAL seed audience — none completed.
Root Cause 04 · High Impact
CRO and checkout fixes never deployed — 65% of checkouts still abandoning
Revenue loss estimate: ₹4–5L/mo based on 80–100 additional purchases at ₹5K CPA
Sheet 14 Section 7 (CRO) and Section 8 (Checkout) contained 14 specific technical interventions. Day 20 evidence shows IC→Purchase at 35% — identical to the Mar+Apr baseline of 36%. Zero improvement. Confirmed unactioned: CO-5 (OTP delivery audit), CO-6 (address autofill), CO-7 (UPI payment ordering), CRO-1 (mobile speed), CRO-2 (checkout audit). The plan estimated recovering 80–100 purchases/month from checkout improvement alone. This is ₹4–5L/month being left on the table every 30 days the fix is delayed.
Root Cause 05 · Medium Impact
Email and WhatsApp revenue channels not operational
Target miss: ₹40–60K/wk email revenue by Day 30 = ₹100% shortfall
Sheet 14 W1 required Klaviyo 6-email post-purchase flow live and BiteSpeed cart abandonment (1hr/24hr/72hr) running. The execution checklist shows BiteSpeed WhatsApp Broadcast noted. However, Klaviyo email flows show no attributed revenue in the period. The 6 planned email flows — welcome, post-purchase D2/D7/D14, cart abandonment, reactivation D60 — would alone account for ₹40–60K/week by Day 30. These are near-zero-CAC revenue that compound with time. Each week they remain undeployed is a permanent loss.
Root Cause 06 · Medium Impact
C2 South cities launched English-only — localization plan not executed
Wasted spend: ₹23,456 in C2-AS1 South at 0 purchases, 0 checkouts
Sheet 15 C2 specification: "ONE localized variant per major city if possible (Tamil/Telugu/Kannada caption overlays for South cities)." Sheet 16 C2-AS1 spec: AD-C2-2 with "Tamil subtitle overlay added via Higgsfield." The ad was created but the Tamil overlay version (AD_C2_AS1_ASTHA-BUNDLE-TAMIL-OVERLAY_v1) spent ₹17,028 with 77,076 impressions and 0 purchases. The English version (AD-C2-1) spent ₹3,431 with 11,335 impressions, 0 purchases. South Indian audiences for fashion brands require vernacular creative — the localization was planned but appears to have been incorrectly implemented (captions may not have been culturally adapted, just translated).
Root Cause 07 · Medium Impact
Bundle attach rate near zero — Core Pair not converting at AOV target
AOV gap: ₹4,626 actual vs ₹7,800 target — ₹3,174 per order missing on C1
The entire strategy was built on bundle AOV: Core Pair at ₹7,999, Premium Trio at ₹11,499. The plan required Core Pair PDP to be live (marked "done" in checklist). However, C1 actual AOV is ₹4,626, indicating buyers are landing on individual product pages, not the bundle page, or they are not taking the bundle offer at the bundle page. The ₹3,174 AOV gap means each purchase generates ₹3,174 less revenue than planned — which explains the ROAS deficit even at the same CPA. Creative CTAs may not be driving to /collections/core-pair as specified.
Root Cause 08 · Structural Impact
New campaigns in learning phase — ROAS unrepresentative at Day 9–20
Misread risk: 0.39× C1 ROAS at Day 9 ≠ campaign failure — Meta needs 50+ purchases
This is a context issue, not a failure. C1 launched May 11 (9 days before the reporting period ends). C2 launched May 13 (7 days). Meta's algorithm requires a minimum of 50 conversion events to exit the learning phase and optimize delivery. At 8 and 4 purchases respectively, both campaigns are mid-learning. The plan accounted for this in its W4 review gate: "If any prospecting ad set <1.3x ROAS at ₹15K spend → kill." C1 and C2 have not reached ₹15K per ad set at 7-day ROAS — the kill rule has not been triggered. Premature budget decisions now would waste the creative and learning investment already made.
